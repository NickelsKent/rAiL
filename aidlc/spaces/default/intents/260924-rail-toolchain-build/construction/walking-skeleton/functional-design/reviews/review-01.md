### Summary

The functional design's workflows, entities and business rules are internally coherent in the large — the WF1–WF6 pipeline, the state machines and the entity/rule cross-references mostly hold up under a grammar-level trace against the spec, and the FR27/BR mapping in `traceability.json` is complete and correct. But two concrete, self-contradicting defects (BR1.6's literal wording rejecting the design's own fixtures, and `traceability.json` mis-tracking FR13.1/FR13.2/NFR3/NFR6/NFR7.1 against this unit's own cited rule sources) and two workflow/rule trigger inconsistencies (BR2.7's trigger field, and WF5's incomplete dispatch enumeration for a repeat `initialize`) mean a developer implementing this document literally would either break on day one or have to guess at behaviour the design claims to have already settled.
