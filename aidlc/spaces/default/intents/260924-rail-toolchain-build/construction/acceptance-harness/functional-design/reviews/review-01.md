### Summary

The design is thorough and its state machines are coherent. The volume ratchet, the pending-suite policy and the exit-code rules are well specified. It is NOT-READY because four gaps would force a developer to guess or would let a suite be weakened: the ratchet ignores the volume unit and exhaustive set (R-01), every level runs a debug `rail` (R-02), T13's reference-machine rule is not enforced (R-03), and cross-platform pass criteria have no comparison mechanism (R-04).
