### Summary

The unit decomposition, its DAG structural mechanics (acyclic, well-typed, well-named), and its requirement traceability are sound and match the plan the human approved. However, cross-referencing the component-level dependency graph from Domain Design against the unit DAG surfaces a genuine, requirements-corroborated gap: several early/mid units (U3, U7, U8, U10) add acceptance suites that the component catalogue and the requirements themselves say run "over the corpus," yet the DAG gives no unit a dependency path to `benchmark-corpus` (U16) before those suites are due — and structurally cannot, since U16 depends on U5 which is itself downstream of U3/U4. This is a build-blocking ordering defect under the team's own "CI must be green before merge" and "never lower a recorded acceptance-suite pass criterion" rules, and the affected units are recommended for revision before this artifact is approved.
